New to this? The four ideas that matter

More than anything you could pick, these four decide how it goes. Everything on this dashboard rests on them.

1

Small amounts become large amounts

Compounding: your money earns returns, and those returns earn returns. A $1,000 paycheck invested today keeps working for decades.

2

Don't bet on one company

Diversification: an index fund holds hundreds of companies at once, so no single disaster can sink you. It's why your core is index funds, not stocks.

3

Your behavior in bad weeks matters most

When the market drops 20%, doing nothing is usually the winning move. What you do in the scary weeks beats anything you pick in the good ones.

4

Time in the market beats timing it

The best days cluster right next to the worst ones — jump out to dodge a drop and you'll miss the rebound too. Staying in, steadily, wins.
